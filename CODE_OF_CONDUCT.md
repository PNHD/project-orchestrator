# Code of Conduct

Project Orchestrator contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, identities, experience levels, and differing viewpoints.
- Share only information and artifacts you are authorized to disclose.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and community health.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. For confidential conduct concerns, use a private repository contact channel when one is published. Do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue.
